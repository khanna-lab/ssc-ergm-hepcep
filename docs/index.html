SSC 2026 Workshop

From Aggregate Network Summaries to Synthetic Networked Populations

Aditya Khanna Brown University School of Public Health

Jonathan Ozik Argonne National Laboratory / University of Chicago

Slides

Rendered decks as presented. Six modules, roughly 30 minutes each.

  1. 00 Preamble Workshop overview, setup, and roadmap
  2. 01 A Brief Introduction to ERGMs Core ideas, using the Add Health friendship network
  3. 02 From Aggregate Summaries to Network Targets Turning published summaries into estimable target statistics
  4. 03 Sequential ERGM Specification Building a model term by term, with warm starts
  5. 04 Assessing Failure Modes Degeneracy, non-convergence, and reading MCMC diagnostics
  6. 05 Simulation as Diagnostic Checking the fitted ensemble against targets, and exporting to an ABM
  7. 06 Geographic Extensions Adding spatial structure with a custom dnf term

Each deck is a self-contained reveal.js file. Press ? for keyboard shortcuts, O for a slide overview, and F for fullscreen.

Code, exercises, and data: github.com/khanna-lab/ssc-ergm-hepcep

A preliminary version of this workflow appears in Boodram et al. (2022), PLOS ONE, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0248850.
