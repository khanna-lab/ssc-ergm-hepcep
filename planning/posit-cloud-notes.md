# Posit Cloud: delivery & pricing notes

- **Free tier:** 25 project hours/month per user. A 3-hour workshop is ~3 hours, so well under the cap. Real limits are session RAM and sharing many people into a Space; our models are small, so sharing-at-scale is the reason to consider paid.
- **For class sharing, use the Instructor plan** ($15/month per instructor, month-to-month, no annual commitment). Run it as a course Space / assignment.
- **Recommended: Instructor metered billing.** Participants stay on free accounts; 300 compute hours included (30 people × 3 hours ≈ 90), so effectively ~$15 for the one month, then cancel.
- Other variants: fee-per-student ($15 + $5/student/mo) and students-pay ($5/student/mo); both pricier for a one-off.
- **Check Brown academic pricing** (Posit Academic) before paying: universities sometimes get instructor access free or discounted.

Sources: [plans](https://posit.cloud/plans/), [instructor plans](https://posit.cloud/plans/instructor), [teach guide](https://docs.posit.co/cloud/guide/teach/), [academic pricing](https://posit.co/pricing/academic)
